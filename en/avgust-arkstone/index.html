Features

Everything you need from AI. Nothing extra on the network

Avgust Arkstone brings several open models under one roof: each does its own job, and you work in one window.

  • Chat

    Answers, explains, translates, writes. Remembers your general rules and facts about you.

    • Sees the pictures you attach
    • Searches the web and gives sources — when you allow it
    • A thinking mode for hard tasks

  • Code

    A separate model for programming. Every project has its own folder and its own history.

    • Files from an answer are written with one button
    • Works with any language

  • Artist

    Image generation with Z-Image and FLUX models right on the Mac’s chip. The chat hands the task to the artist by itself.

    • Aspect ratio, resolution and file format
    • The file name carries the description and date

  • Knowledge base

    Add your PDF, DOCX, EPUB or notes — the chat will look for answers in them, even offline.

    • Ready packs: Wikipedia by topic, survival and first-aid manuals
    • Open sources only, with the author credited

  • Model catalog

    Avgust Arkstone looks at your Mac and suggests models that will run on it. One-button install with an integrity check.

    • Updates and removal without leftovers
    • Search across thousands of Hugging Face models

  • Cloud — if you want it

    Need a stronger model than your Mac can run? Turn on “Cloud” and it answers right away, with no sign-up and no keys. The built-in FreeLLMAPI connects free cloud models, and your own free keys from Google, Groq, OpenRouter and others add stronger ones. Off by default — offline stays first.

    • Picks a provider itself and switches when a limit runs out
    • Works without keys; your own keys are stored encrypted on your disk

  • Lives on a disk

    Put Avgust Arkstone on an external SSD — models, history, memory and projects stay together. Plug it into another Mac and keep working.

    • Updates without losing data

Privacy

Your data goes nowhere

Internet is off by default

The models run on your Mac. Not a single request goes out until you turn the internet on yourself — to download models, updates or search. Cloud models have their own switch, and only with it do questions go to the chosen provider.

Overload protection

Avgust Arkstone will not start a model that does not fit in memory, and stops it by itself if the Mac overheats or starts to hang. You can turn the protection off — at your own risk.

Install

Three steps to the first answer

  1. Drag Avgust Arkstone.app into a folder

    Create a folder on an external disk or anywhere on your Mac, for example “Arkstone”, and drag Avgust Arkstone.app into it — the data is stored next to the app. Applications works too — then the data goes to ~/Arkstone.

  2. Allow the first launch

    Open Avgust Arkstone.app. If macOS blocks it: System Settings → Privacy & Security → “Open Anyway”. You only do this once.

  3. Pick the models

    Avgust Arkstone detects your Mac and offers a starter set. Press “Download selected” — once it finishes, you can turn the internet off.

Why does macOS warn you? Avgust Arkstone is not yet certified by Apple — that is a paid procedure for developers. The app sends no data to the network and does not touch files outside its own folders without your permission.

Questions

Do I need the internet?

Only to download models and updates. After that Avgust Arkstone works fully offline. Web search and cloud models are turned on separately when you need them.

Which Mac do I need?

Any Mac with Apple silicon — M1, M2, M3, M4 and later. Light models run on 8 GB of memory, bigger ones on 16 GB and more. Avgust Arkstone tells you what fits.

Where is my data stored?

In a folder next to the app: models, chats, memory, projects and pictures. Nothing is sent to servers. To move to another disk, just copy the Arkstone, Projects and Images folders.

Where do the models come from?

From open sources — mostly Hugging Face. Every file is checked against its checksum; a damaged one will not install.

Why won’t macOS open the app?

Avgust Arkstone is not yet certified by Apple. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security and press “Open Anyway”. You do this once on each Mac.

Will there be an iPhone or Windows version?

An iPhone version is in progress. Windows is not planned yet.
